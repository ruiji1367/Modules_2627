What is a design pattern?

A design pattern is a reusable design idea for a recurring software-design problem — not a piece of code you copy and paste. It is a general way of organizing classes and objects, and you adapt it to your own application.

"An architectural blueprint is not the finished house. It is a guide for how the house can be designed."
